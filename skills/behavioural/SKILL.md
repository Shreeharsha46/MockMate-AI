# Behavioral Interview Skill

## STAR Method Rubric

This skill provides a structured framework for evaluating behavioral interview responses using the STAR method.

### What is STAR?

STAR stands for:
- **S**ituation: Describe the context or background
- **T**ask: Explain the challenge or responsibility
- **A**ction: Detail what you specifically did
- **R**esult: Share the outcome and what you learned

### Evaluation Criteria

| Criterion | Description | Score Weight |
|-----------|-------------|--------------|
| **Situation Clarity** | Is the context clearly described? | 15% |
| **Task Understanding** | Does the candidate identify their specific role? | 20% |
| **Action Specificity** | Are actions concrete and personal ("I" not "we")? | 30% |
| **Result Quantification** | Are results measurable and impact shown? | 25% |
| **Reflection/Learning** | Did they extract lessons learned? | 10% |

### Sample Questions

1. Tell me about a time you faced a challenge at work.
2. Describe a situation where you worked in a team.
3. What is your greatest professional achievement?
4. Tell me about a time you had to learn a new technology quickly.
5. Describe a conflict you had with a colleague and how you resolved it.
6. Tell me about a time you failed and what you learned.

### Scoring Guide

- **9-10**: Exceptional - Clear STAR structure, quantified results, strong reflection
- **7-8**: Good - Complete STAR elements, minor gaps in specificity
- **5-6**: Adequate - Partial STAR coverage, vague actions or results
- **0-4**: Poor - Missing key elements, unclear or irrelevant response

### Feedback Template

When evaluating a behavioral response, provide:

1. **Strengths**: What was done well (exactly 2)
2. **Gaps**: What's missing or weak (exactly 2)
3. **STAR Audit**: Which element(s) were weak/missing
4. **Hint**: One actionable suggestion for improvement
5. **Next Topic**: Related behavioral theme to explore next
